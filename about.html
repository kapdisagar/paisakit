About PaisaKit

PaisaKit is a collection of fast, free, privacy-first finance tools built for India.

Why we exist

Try searching "SIP calculator" and you land on pages buried under ads, popup newsletters and slow scripts. PaisaKit does one thing: give you the number, instantly. No sign-up, no tracking of what you type, no clutter.

How it works

Every tool is plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript that runs in your browser. Nothing you enter is sent to any server. This also makes the site extremely fast on slow mobile connections — which is how most of India browses.

Roadmap

New tools are added every week: more calculators, Hindi and Gujarati versions, and utility tools. Have a suggestion? We're listening.

Honesty policy

All our results are estimates based on standard public formulas. We link to sources where relevant and we never pretend a calculation is "your" bank's exact quote. Nothing on PaisaKit is financial advice.
