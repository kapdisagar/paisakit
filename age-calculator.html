How age is counted

The conventional method: count full years completed, then full months since the last birthday, then remaining days. This matches how documents, exams and forms in India count age.

FAQ

Why do months have different lengths in the result?

Months are counted calendar-wise (e.g. 15 Jan → 15 Feb = 1 month), not as fixed 30-day blocks. That's why day counts can vary between two people of the "same age".

How does India count age for exams and documents?

Most government forms use completed years as on a cut-off date. Some (like NEET's minimum age) specify exact dates — always check the specific notification.
