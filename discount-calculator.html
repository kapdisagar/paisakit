How discount is calculated

Basic discount math:

You pay = Price × (1 − Discount% ÷ 100)

Example: ₹1,999 par 30% off → discount = ₹599.70, aap pay karenge ₹1,399. Real discount wapas nikalne ke liye: (1 − paid ÷ original) × 100.

Stacked discounts ka dhyan rakhein: "30% off + extra 10% off" 40% nahi hota. Pehle 30% lagta hai, phir 10% bache hue price par: ₹1,999 → ₹1,399 → ₹1,259. Total sirf 37% off hai. Formula: 1 − (1−d₁)(1−d₂).

FAQ

50% off + 50% off = free?

Nahi. Do baar 50% ka matlab hai price ka 0.5 × 0.5 = 0.25 bachna — yaani total 75% off, poora free nahi. Stacked offers hamesha ek-ek karke bache hue price par lagte hain.

Sites MRP badha ke discount kyun dikhati hain?

Yeh common trick hai — sale se pehle price badha kar bada "% off" dikhana. Bachav ke liye: apne paas pura screenshot rakhein, price-history websites check karein, aur upar wale "real discount" box mein claimed MRP vs paid price daal kar sach dekhein.

Bank offer aur cashback ka kya karun?

Instant bank discount final price ko turant kam karta hai — usse paid price mein gina ja sakta hai. Cashback wallet credit hota hai with conditions, isliye guaranteed savings nahi. Dono ko alag treat karein aur sirf instant discount ko real savings count karein.

Kya displayed price par aur kuch pay karna padega?

India mein MRP mein GST already included hota hai. Online par delivery fee, handling fee ya surge pricing alag se jud sakti hai — checkout tak ka final amount hi "you pay" hai, wahi use karein.
