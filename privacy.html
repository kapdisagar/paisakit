Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 September 2026

The short version

Every calculator on PaisaKit runs entirely inside your browser. The numbers you enter never leave your device — we do not have servers that receive them, and we do not store, track or sell anything you type.

What we collect

Nothing by default. No accounts, no forms, no cookies set by us. Standard anonymous server logs (like page views) may be maintained by our hosting provider for security and traffic purposes; these cannot identify you personally.

Advertising

If this site displays advertisements in the future (for example, via Google AdSense), third-party vendors including Google may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites. You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated date above will change with it.

Questions? Reach us via the About page.
